# RunenSpatial Licensing

## Current public license

The current RunenSpatial revision is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 only (`GPL-3.0-only`). The complete public license text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Separate commercial licensing

A separate commercial license may be available from copyright holder(s) with sufficient rights to grant it. Commercial terms are separate from the GPL public license and are not represented by an SPDX identifier in this repository.

Repository ownership, organization membership, or maintainer status does not by itself establish authority to relicense every copyright interest. Any commercial grant extends only to material for which the granting party or parties hold sufficient rights.

## Historical revisions

This licensing change is prospective. Revisions made available before the Dornglut licensing-policy migration remain available under the license terms granted on those revisions, including the prior `MIT OR Apache-2.0` terms. Rights already granted for those revisions are not revoked by the current migration.

Git history preserves the prior license texts and the repository's transfer history.

## Third-party material

Third-party dependencies and any incorporated third-party material retain their separately applicable licenses, notices, and attribution requirements. The RunenSpatial license change does not relabel third-party material or represent it as covered by a Dornglut commercial grant where Dornglut lacks sufficient relicensing rights.

## Contributions

Separate commercial licensing requires sufficient rights over accepted copyright-bearing repository content.

Until a reviewed inbound contribution mechanism establishes those rights, external pull requests that add tracked repository content, including code, documentation, tests, examples, build scripts, or assets, are not accepted. Issue reports, design discussion, reviews, reproducible cases supplied through discussion, and other participation that does not add third-party repository content remain available under repository governance.

A future CLA, copyright assignment, or other inbound licensing mechanism requires separate review before this restriction changes.
